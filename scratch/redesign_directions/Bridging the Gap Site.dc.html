The privacy promise

Your words belong to you.

Trust is the whole product. Here is exactly how information moves — in plain language, no legal maze.

Who can read what you share

Only the counselor or trusted adult assigned to your case. Role-based access means teachers, other students, and administrators see nothing unless you or your family agree.

When others are told

Parents and caregivers are involved appropriately, with your input. Community partners are invited only with explicit family consent. Law enforcement only when someone is in danger and the law requires it.

What the app never does

No location tracking. No monitoring of your phone, messages, or social media. No cameras. No selling data — ever. It only knows what you choose to type into it.

The paper trail protects you

Every action on a case is documented and auditable, FERPA-aligned. You can see who read your words and when. Accountability is built in, not bolted on.

Questions about privacy?

Ask us directly — a person will answer. Schools and districts can request the full compliance documentation.
