# Parent Guide 3 — Food & Basic Needs Support

This guide is informational only and does not provide medical, legal, or mental
health advice. If you believe your family is in immediate danger, contact
emergency services or a qualified professional.

## Start with school-based support

- Ask about free or reduced meal programs.
- Look for weekend backpack food programs.
- Request a private check-in with a counselor or social worker.

## Community resources

- Local food pantries and meal programs.
- Community resource hotlines (non-emergency).
- Neighborhood or faith-based support programs.

## Supportive language to use

- "You do not have to handle this alone."
- "It is okay to ask for help."
- "We can take one small step at a time."
