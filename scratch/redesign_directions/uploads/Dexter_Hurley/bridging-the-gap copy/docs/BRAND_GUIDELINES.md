## 📋 Brand Overview

### Official Brand Name
```
Bridging the Gap™ – School Safety
```

### Mission Statement
Connecting students, families, schools, and safety partners before concerns become crises.

### Core Value Proposition
A prevention-focused, role-based school safety and wellness platform that helps students speak up, educators respond appropriately, families stay informed, and School Resource Officers receive timely awareness of serious threats — all while respecting privacy, policy, and care-first intervention.

---

## 🎨 Logo Direction

### Visual Concept
**Symbol:** A stylized bridge arc connecting two shapes (people or shield forms), symbolizing communication and early intervention.

### Visual Meaning
- **Bridge** = Connection between students and help
- **Gap** = Unmet needs and silence (what the platform closes)
- **Arc** = Protection, support, and prevention

### Design Specifications

#### Color Palette
| Color | Use | Hex | Purpose |
|-------|-----|-----|---------|
| **Deep Blue** | Primary | `#1E3A8A` | Trust, safety, authority |
| **Warm Orange** | Secondary | `#F97316` | Urgency, care, action |
| **Neutral Tan** | Background | `#F5F1EA` | Calm, clarity, accessibility |
| **White** | Text/Contrast | `#FFFFFF` | Clean, professional |

#### Typography
- **Font Family:** Sans-serif, rounded edges
- **Style:** Clean, modern, district-safe
- **Avoid:** Aggressive or "security-only" fonts
- **Recommended:** Inter, Poppins, or similar accessible sans-serif

#### Logo Usage Rules
- ✅ Use on white or light backgrounds
- ✅ Maintain clear space around symbol (minimum 1/8" or 20% of logo height)
- ✅ Use full color version in print/digital
- ✅ Use monochrome (blue) when color unavailable
- ❌ Do NOT rotate, stretch, or distort
- ❌ Do NOT add drop shadows or effects
- ❌ Do NOT place on busy backgrounds

## 🏛️ Brand Usage Rules (Trademark-Safe)

### Always Use Full Name In:
- ✅ Contracts
- ✅ Grant applications
- ✅ Board presentations
- ✅ Website headers
- ✅ Legal documents
- ✅ Formal communications

**Correct Format:**
```
Bridging the Gap™ – School Safety
```

### Acceptable Short Form (Informal Only)
- In conversation/email: "Bridging the Gap™"
- In headings (secondary): "Bridging the Gap"
- Do NOT use short form in:
  - Contracts ❌
  - Legal documents ❌
  - Grant applications ❌
  - Official board materials ❌

### DO NOT:
- ❌ Use "Bridging the Gap" alone in legal documents
- ❌ Abbreviate to "BTG" in contracts
- ❌ Combine brand with unrelated logos or causes
- ❌ Modify the name or add suffix (e.g., "Bridging the Gap Plus")
- ❌ Claim medical or law-enforcement authority

### Trademark Symbol Usage

| Stage | Symbol | Example | Status |
|-------|--------|---------|--------|
| **Current (Pre-Registration)** | ™ | Bridging the Gap™ | In public use, unregistered |
| **After USPTO Approval** | ® | Bridging the Gap® | Federally registered |
| **Pending Application** | TM | Bridging the Gap | Temporary (avoid) |

**Rule:** Use ™ publicly now; will convert to ® after USPTO approval (typically 12–18 months).

## 🚫 What NOT to Do

### Brand Violations (Avoid)
- ❌ "Saving Grace" (old brand — completely retire)
- ❌ "BTG School Safety" (abbreviation not permitted)
- ❌ "Bridging the Gap Safety" (incorrect name order)
- ❌ "Bridging the Gap for Schools" (adds unnecessary suffix)
- ❌ Combining logo with school district logo (separate clearly)

### Marketing Violations
- ❌ Claiming medical diagnoses or treatment
- ❌ Claiming law-enforcement authority
- ❌ Using in political campaigns or controversial causes
- ❌ Licensing to non-education sectors without approval

### Legal Violations
- ❌ Trademark symbol (™) in contracts before registration
- ❌ Claiming "federal trademark" before USPTO approval
- ❌ Using registered mark (®) before approved
- ❌ Modifying the name to suit local preferences
