# Parent Guide 1 — Bullying Conversation

This guide is informational only and does not provide medical, legal, or mental
health advice. If you believe your child may be in immediate danger, contact
emergency services or a qualified professional.

## Start with calm, open questions

- "How have things been feeling at school lately?"
- "Is there anything that has been making school harder?"
- "Who do you feel comfortable talking to?"
- "Has anything happened that made you feel uncomfortable or upset?"

Tip: Ask one question at a time and listen more than you speak.

## Helpful responses

- "Thank you for telling me."
- "I am really glad you shared this."
- "We can figure this out together."

## Try to avoid

- "Why did you not tell me sooner?"
- "Just ignore it."
- "That does not sound like a big deal."

## Supportive next steps

- Continue checking in at home.
- Reach out to a school counselor or trusted staff member.
- Use school resources to request guidance.
- Review support options together.

You do not need to solve everything at once.
