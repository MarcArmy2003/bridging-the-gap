## 🖼 Logo Specifications

### Main Logo: Bridging the Gap

**Concept:**
- Clean bridge icon connecting two pillars
- Subtle shield shape integrated behind the bridge
- Bridge in Bridge Blue (#1F5FAF)
- Shield in Deep Navy (#0E2A47)
- Optional gold line underneath for premium look

**Dimensions:**
- Logo Height: 60px (min), 120px (preferred)
- Padding: 20px minimum clear space on all sides
- Aspect Ratio: ~3:1 (horizontal)

**Text Lockup:**
```
[Bridge Icon] Bridging the Gap
             School Safety
```

**Typography (Logo):**
- Wordmark: Sans-serif, modern
- "Bridging the Gap" — Bold, 24pt+
- "School Safety" — Regular, 16pt, subtitle

### Color Variations

| Context | Logo Color | Background | Use Case |
|---------|-----------|------------|----------|
| **Primary** | Bridge Blue + Navy | White or Light Gray | All primary uses |
| **White** | White with outline | Deep Navy background | Premium sections, dark backgrounds |
| **Monochrome** | Deep Navy | White | Print, fax, black & white docs |
| **Gold** | Gold outline, Navy fill | White | Awards, certificates, achievement |

### Logo Usage Rules

✅ **DO:**
- Maintain clear space (20px minimum)
- Use on white or light backgrounds primarily
- Keep proportions locked (don't stretch)
- Use high-resolution files (SVG preferred)
- Maintain contrast ratios (WCAG AA minimum)

❌ **DON'T:**
- Rotate or skew the logo
- Add effects (shadows, glows, etc.)
- Change colors without approval
- Place on busy/patterned backgrounds
- Abbreviate to "BTG" in official materials

## 🎨 Visual Design Guidelines

### Typography

**Primary Font: Inter (or Poppins)**
- Clean, modern, professional
- Rounded edges preferred
- Weights: Regular (400), Medium (500), Bold (700)

**Font Sizes & Hierarchy:**
```
Hero Headline:        48–64px Bold
Subheadline:         24–32px Regular
Section Heading:     28–36px Bold
Subsection Title:    18–24px Bold
Body Text:           16px Regular
Small Text/Labels:   12–14px Regular
```

**Line Height:**
- Headlines: 1.2
- Body: 1.6
- Labels: 1.4

### Spacing & Layout

**Grid System:** 8px base unit
```
Padding: 16px, 24px, 32px, 48px (multiples of 8)
Margins: 16px, 24px, 32px, 48px
Border Radius: 4px, 8px, 12px (for cards/buttons)
```

**Component Spacing:**
- Card padding: 24px
- Section margins: 48px
- Button padding: 12px vertical, 24px horizontal

### Button Styles

**Primary Button:**
- Background: Bridge Blue (#1F5FAF)
- Text: White
- Padding: 12px 24px
- Border Radius: 8px
- Font Weight: Bold

**Secondary Button:**
- Background: Light Gray (#F4F6F8)
- Text: Deep Navy (#0E2A47)
- Border: 2px Bridge Blue (#1F5FAF)
- Padding: 12px 24px
- Border Radius: 8px

**Alert Button (Critical Only):**
- Background: Alert Red (#C62828)
- Text: White
- Only for life-safety escalations
