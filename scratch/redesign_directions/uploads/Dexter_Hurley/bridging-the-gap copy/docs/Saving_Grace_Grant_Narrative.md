# Saving Grace — Grant Narrative (Draft)

Saving Grace is a school safety and well-being platform designed to help
students, families, and schools respond to concerns before they become crises.
The platform centers on calm, supportive guidance; role-based access; and clear
boundaries that protect student privacy.

## Problem Statement

Schools and districts are seeing rising concerns related to bullying, stress,
and basic needs. These concerns are often reported late, handled inconsistently,
or escalated without clear coordination. Families want support and guidance,
and staff need tools that reduce confusion rather than add complexity.

## Proposed Solution

Saving Grace provides:

- Confidential reporting pathways for students.
- Guided, step-by-step support for families.
- Consistent workflows for counselors and school staff.
- Educational guidance for School Resource Officers when involved.

The system is designed to be trauma-informed and school-appropriate without
providing medical, legal, or diagnostic advice.

## Intended Outcomes

- Earlier identification of concerns.
- Reduced escalation through clear next steps.
- Improved family trust and communication.
- Support for counselor workload planning.
- Privacy-protected, aggregated reporting for district leadership.

## Equity and Access

Saving Grace supports students across a range of needs, including bullying,
emotional well-being, and food insecurity. Resources are written in plain
language and designed to reduce barriers to asking for help.

## Implementation Plan

Phase 1: Alignment and readiness
- Confirm roles and escalation boundaries.
- Review privacy language and district policy alignment.
- Identify pilot schools and staff leads.

Phase 2: Pilot rollout
- Train staff on response workflows.
- Share student and family resources.
- Monitor feedback and adjust guidance.

Phase 3: District expansion
- Extend to additional schools.
- Review aggregate metrics for board reporting.
- Maintain privacy and role-based access.

## Sustainability

The platform reduces manual coordination and provides repeatable, documented
workflows. Districts can scale the program over time and use aggregated insights
to guide staffing and support decisions.

## Privacy & Safety

Saving Grace uses role-based access and minimal data collection. Information is
never shared publicly. The platform does not provide medical, legal, or mental
health advice and does not replace emergency services.
