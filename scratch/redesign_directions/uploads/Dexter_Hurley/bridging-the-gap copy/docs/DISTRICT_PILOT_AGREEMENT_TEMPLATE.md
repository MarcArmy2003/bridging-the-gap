## 1. PROGRAM OVERVIEW

### 1.1 Purpose
Bridging the Gap: School Safety is a structured incident management and school safety platform designed to help districts:
- Collect incident reports from students and staff
- Route concerns to appropriate counselors
- Track and document intervention workflows
- Escalate critical threats per district policy
- Maintain audit-ready documentation

### 1.2 Scope of Pilot
- **Duration:** 90 days from date above
- **Schools Participating:** 1–3 schools
- **Staff Accounts:** Up to 50 (counselors, administrators, optional SRO)
- **Demo Feature:** Pre-loaded demo threat scenarios for training
- **Training:** One (1) in-person or virtual training session (2 hours)

### 1.3 What This IS
- A workflow and documentation tool
- Support for counselor decision-making
- An audit trail for district records
- A communication platform between students and staff
- Optional integration with district systems

### 1.4 What This IS NOT
- A replacement for district policy
- An automated threat detection system
- A law enforcement tool
- A substitute for professional judgment
- A medical/mental health diagnosis tool

## 4. DATA & PRIVACY

### 4.1 FERPA Compliance
This pilot is designed to comply with FERPA (Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act). Provider will:
- [ ] Store all student data in secure, encrypted format
- [ ] Limit access to authorized staff only
- [ ] Maintain audit logs of all data access
- [ ] Disable demo data before any real student reports

### 4.2 Data Ownership
- District retains full ownership of all data entered into the system
- Provider will not use district data for marketing or product development
- Upon pilot completion, district may export all data
- Provider will delete all pilot data within 30 days of agreement termination (unless extended)

### 4.3 Data Security
Provider commits to:
- [ ] End-to-end encryption for data in transit
- [ ] AES-256 encryption for data at rest
- [ ] SOC 2 Type II compliance (or similar)
- [ ] Annual third-party security audit
- [ ] 60-day notification of any data breach

### 4.4 Student Consent & Notifications
Provider recommends:
- District provides parental/student notification
- District publishes privacy policy for platform
- District obtains board approval before pilot
- District communicates purpose: "Non-punitive support and safety coordination"

## 5. ACCEPTABLE USE

### 5.1 Permitted Uses
District staff may use the platform to:
- Report student concerns (bullying, mental health, safety)
- Document counselor support plans
- Track intervention outcomes
- Escalate credible threats per district policy
- Maintain audit trails for compliance

### 5.2 Prohibited Uses
District staff may NOT use the platform to:
- Conduct surveillance of students
- Profiles students based on demographics
- Diagnose medical or psychological conditions
- Share data with law enforcement without legal process
- Use for disciplinary purposes only (must be support-focused)

### 5.3 Escalation Protocols
- District retains full authority over escalation decisions
- Provider does NOT automatically alert law enforcement
- District determines SRO involvement threshold
- District determines parent/guardian notification protocols

### 10.1 Termination for Convenience
Either party may terminate with:
- 30 days written notice
- No penalty (pilot fees keep access through notice period)

- No refund of pilot fees

### 10.2 Termination for Cause
Provider may terminate if:
- District violates acceptable use policy (Section 5.2)
- District uses system for unlawful purposes
- District fails to pay fees (10 days after notice)
