# Parent Guide 2 — School Stress & Well-Being

This guide is informational only and does not provide medical, legal, or mental
health advice. If you believe your child may be in immediate danger, contact
emergency services or a qualified professional.

## Gentle check-in questions

- "What part of your day feels the most stressful?"
- "What helps you feel calm when you are overwhelmed?"
- "Is there anything you wish adults understood better?"

Reassurance: Children do not always have the words. Patience matters.

## Supportive routines

- Keep regular sleep and meal routines.
- Encourage short breaks and quiet time.
- Add movement: walking, stretching, or light exercise.

## When to seek school support

- Stress is persistent or affecting school participation.
- Your child asks for help or seems overwhelmed.
- You want guidance on next steps.

Schools can coordinate support with counselors and trusted staff members.
