             before concerns become crises.

CTAs: "See How It Works" + "For Schools & Districts"

### How It Works
1. **Students Report Early** — Confidential concerns
2. **Counselors Guide Response** — Triage and support
3. **Parents Stay Informed** — Updates and resources
4. **Safety Partners Alerted** — SRO escalation per policy

### Footer Disclaimer
```
Bridging the Gap™ is a support and prevention platform. 
It does not provide medical, legal, or law-enforcement advice 
and operates in alignment with district-approved safety protocols.
```

## 💼 Sales Pitch At A Glance

### One-Minute Elevator Pitch
```
Bridging the Gap™ – School Safety is a prevention-focused platform 
that helps districts identify concerns early, coordinate support 
efficiently, and respond appropriately to serious threats. The system 
improves communication between students, counselors, teachers, parents, 
and School Resource Officers while reducing staff burnout and maintaining 
policy compliance.
```

### Key Benefits
✅ Early identification of bullying, mental health, safety concerns  
✅ Counselor-led workflows with built-in guidance  
✅ Parent engagement without oversharing  
✅ Direct escalation for high-risk threats  
✅ Demo and presentation modes for boards  
✅ Designed for K–12 with non-device options for K–5
