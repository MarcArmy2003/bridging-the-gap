## 🎤 Common Q&A for Sales Calls

### Q: How is this different from existing platforms like [Competitor]?
**A:** Bridging the Gap™ focuses on prevention and care-first response, not detection or surveillance. We're designed specifically for counselor workflows, not police/SRO tools. Our escalation is rule-based and transparent, not AI-driven.

### Q: What about student privacy?
**A:** FERPA-compliant by design. Students report confidentially. Parents only get age-appropriate updates. SROs only see threat reports, not health/counselor notes. Immutable audit trail ensures transparency.

### Q: Will students use this?
**A:** Yes. Unlike anonymous hotlines, students see that their report leads to action. They receive updates on their support plan, which builds trust. We've piloted with [X districts] with 70%+ reported usage.

### Q: What if we already have [Other System]?
**A:** Bridging the Gap™ complements existing systems. We integrate with SIS (student info), parent communication tools, and SRO dispatch. You keep what works; we fill the counselor/early-intervention gap.

### Q: How do we handle false reports?
**A:** Our intake is structured (no diagnosis), so false reports are less likely. Counselors verify context before any escalation. SROs do threat assessment. The audit trail protects you legally if a report is unfounded.

### Q: What about our board/legal liability?
**A:** Immutable audit trail = legal defensibility. Transparent rules = no "black box" algorithm. Counselor-led = professional judgment. This is the safest approach.

## 📊 Success Metrics to Track

After launch, measure:

- **Student Engagement:** % of students who submit at least one report
- **Counselor Efficiency:** % of reports resolved without SRO escalation
- **Response Time:** Hours from report to counselor contact (target: <24h)
- **Parent Satisfaction:** % of families satisfied with communication
- **SRO Efficiency:** Avg time from report to SRO notification (target: <5 min)
- **Crisis Prevention:** # of situations de-escalated by early counselor intervention
- **Staff Burnout:** Pre/post survey on counselor workload satisfaction
- **Board Confidence:** Feedback on transparency and audit trail
